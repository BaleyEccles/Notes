* Burst Error Correcting Codes
** Interleaving
Take the code words and transmit the nth bit of each code word, now burst errors become bit errors

** Reed-Solomon Codes (RS)
 - Subset of BCH codes
\[(n,k) = (2^m - 1, 2^m - 1 - 2t)\]
 - $t$ is the number of errors to fix
Generator polynomial
\[g(x) = g_0 + g_1X^1 + g_2X^2 + \hdots X^{2t}\]
$g(x)$ roots $\{\alpha^1, \alpha^2, \hdots, \alpha^{2t}\}$
\[g(x) = (X - \alpha^1)(X - \alpha^2)(x - \alpha^3)\hdots(X - \alpha^{2t})\]

*** Exmaple
$RS(7,3)$
\[g(x) = \alpha^3 + \alpha^1 X + \alpha^0 X^2 + \alpha^3 X^3 + X^4\]
Message 010 110 111
$\alpha^1 = 010$
$\alpha^3 = 110$
$\alpha^5 = 111$
\[m(x) = \alpha^1 + \alpha^3 X + \alpha^5X^2\]
\[p(x) = X^{n-k}m(x) \mod g(x)\]
\[p(x) = \alpha^0 + \alpha^2 X + \alpha^4 X^2 + \alpha^6 X^3\]
\[c(x) = \underbrace{\alpha^0 + \alpha^2 X + \alpha^4 X^2 + \alpha^6 X^3} + \underbrace{\alpha^1 X^4 + \alpha^3 X^5 + \alpha^5 X^4}\]

*** Chien Search
Error locator polynomial
\[\sigma (x) = (1 + \beta_1 X)(1 + \beta_2 X)\hdots (1 + \beta_v X)\]
 - $\beta_n$ is the error locations
 - $v$ is number of errors
$\left\{\frac{1}{\beta_1}, \frac{1}{\beta_2}, \hdots, \frac{1}{\beta_v}\right\}$ roots of $\sigma(t)$
\[\begin{bmatrix}
S_1 & S_2 & \hdots & S_{t - 1} & S_{t} \\
S_2 & S_3 & \hdots & S_{t} & S_{t + 1} \\
\vdots & \vdots & \hdots & \vdots & \vdots \\
S_t & S_{t + 1} & \hdots & \hdots & S_{2t - 1} \\
\end{bmatrix}\]
