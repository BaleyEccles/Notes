* Channel Capacity
** Example
$a \rightarrow a$ and $a \rightarrow b$
$b \rightarrow b$ and $b \rightarrow c$
$c \rightarrow c$ and $c \rightarrow d$
$d \rightarrow d$ and $d \rightarrow a$
With probabilities of $0.5$
If we say we cannot receive a $b$ or $d$. This means if we receive a $b$, then it must be an $a$ and if we receive a $d$, then it must be an $c$.
$a \rightarrow a \rightarrow a$
$b \rightarrow b \rightarrow a$
$c \rightarrow c \rightarrow c$
$d \rightarrow d \rightarrow c$

This is not possible to do for a [[id:9a3a019a-1b55-42fc-b4e2-b3c5ddc5b001][Binary Symmetric Channel (BSC)]], the entropy becomes zero.
