* Hamming Distance
amount of symobls/bits that need to change from $A\rightarrow B$

** Example
$1001 \rightarrow 1010$
has Hamming distance of 2:
$10\boxed{0}\boxed{1} \rightarrow 10\boxed{1}\boxed{0}$
