* [[id:205304a8-4440-4708-bc6b-054ff2cbdd79][Noise]] Figure
 - $T_0 = 290\ \textrm{K}$
Measures how much a device degrades [[id:13d613eb-9630-41af-ab3f-c15eabc686f5][SNR]]
\[F = \frac{S_i/N_i}{S_o/N_o}\]
 - $N_i = kT_0 B$


** Noise Figure and Noise Temperature
\[F = 1 + \frac{T_e}{T_0}\]

** Of a lossy transmission line
With $G < 1$:
\[L = \frac{1}{G}\]
\[T_e = (L-1)T\]
\[F = 1 + (L - 1)\frac{T}{T_0}\]

** Cascaded Noise Figuree
For two stages:
\[T_{cas} = T_{e1} + \frac{T_{e2}}{G_1}\]
For multiple stages
\[T_{cas} = T_{e1} + \frac{T_{e2}}{G_1} + \frac{T_{e3}}{G_1G_2} + \hdots\] 

Friis Noise Equation:
For two stages
\[F_{cas} = F_1 + \frac{F_2 - 1}{G_1}\]
For multiple stages
\[F_{cas} = F_1 + \frac{F_2 - 1}{G_1} + \frac{F_3 - 1}{G_1G_2} + \hdots\]
