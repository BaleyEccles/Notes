** Insertion Loss Method
Filter response is defined by its insertion loss / power loss ratio:
\[P_{LR} = \frac{P_{\mathrm{source}}}{P_{\mathrm{load}}} = \frac{P_{\mathrm{inc}}}{P_{\mathrm{load}}} = \frac{1}{1 - |\Gamma(\omega)|^2}\]
\[IL = 10\log_{10}(P_{LR})\]

If the load and source matched: $IL = \frac{1}{|S_{12}|^2}$

\[|\gamma(\omega)|^2 = \frac{M(\omega^2)}{M(\omega^2) + N(\omega^2)}\]
 - $M$ and $N$ are real polynomials of $\omega^2$
\[P_{LR} = 1 + \frac{M(\omega^2)}{N(\omega^2)}\]

** Capacitors and Inductors
\[L_k = \frac{Z_0g_k}{\omega_c\]
\[C_k = \frac{g_k}{Z_0\omega_c}\]

** Transformations
\[\Delta = \frac{\omega_2 - \omega_1}{\omega_0}\]
\[\omega_0 = \sqrt{\omega_1\omega_2}\]
*** High Pass
\[L_{LP} \rightarrow C_{HP} = \frac{1}{Z_0\omega_c g_k}\]
\[C_{LP} \rightarrow L_{HP} = \frac{Z_0}{\omega_c g_k}\]
*** Band Pass
\[L_{LP} \rightarrow L_{BP} &= \frac{g_kZ_0}{\omega_0 \Delta} +  C_{BP} &= \frac{\Delta}{Z_0\omega_0 g_k}\]
\[C_{LP} \rightarrow L_{BP} &= \frac{\Delta Z_0}{\omega_0 g_k} || C_{BP} &= \frac{g_k}{Z_0\omega_0 \Delta}\]
*** Band Stop
\[L_{LP} \rightarrow L_{BS} &= \frac{Z_0g_k\Delta}{\omega_0} ||   C_{BS} &= \frac{1}{Z_0\omega_0 g_k \Delta}\]
\[C_{LP} \rightarrow L_{BS} &= \frac{Z_0}{\omega_0 g_k \Delta} + C_{BS} &= \frac{g_k \Delta}{Z_0\omega_0}\]
