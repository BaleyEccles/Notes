* Question 1
** a
\[Z_{in} = Z_{0}\frac{Z_L + jZ_0\tan(\beta l)}{Z_0 + jZ_L\tan(\beta l)}\]
Where:
\[\beta = \frac{2\pi}{\lambda}\]
** b
\begin{align*}
Z_{in} &= Z_{0}\frac{Z_L + jZ_0\tan(\beta l)}{Z_0 + jZ_L\tan(\beta l)} \\
Z_L &\rightarrow \infty \\
\Rightarrow \frac{Z_0}{Z_L} &\rightarrow 0 \\
\Rightarrow Z_{in} &= \frac{Z_0}{j\tan(\beta l)} \\
\textrm{Sub in } l &= \frac{\lambda}{8} \textrm{ and } \beta = \frac{2\pi}{\lambda} \\
Z_{in} &= \frac{Z_0}{j\tan\left(\frac{\pi}{4}\right)} \\
&= -jZ_0
\end{align*}

** c
From the textbook we get these equations:
\begin{align*}
S_{11} & = \frac{A + \frac{B}{Z_0} - CZ_0 - D}{A + \frac{B}{Z_0} + CZ_0 + D} \\
S_{12} & = \frac{2(AD - BC)}{A + \frac{B}{Z_0} + CZ_0 + D} \\
S_{21} & = \frac{2}{A + \frac{B}{Z_0} + CZ_0 + D} \\
S_{11} & = \frac{-A + \frac{B}{Z_0} - CZ_0 + D}{A + \frac{B}{Z_0} + CZ_0 + D} \\
\end{align*}
With $Z_0 = 1$ and the previous values for $A,B,C,D$ we get:
\[S = \begin{bmatrix}
0 & -\frac{1 + j}{\sqrt{2}} \\
-\frac{1 + j}{\sqrt{2}} & 0 \\
\end{bmatrix}\]
#+BEGIN_SRC octave :results output :exports none :session Q2 :eval no-export :tangle ENG439_Week_3_Q2.m
