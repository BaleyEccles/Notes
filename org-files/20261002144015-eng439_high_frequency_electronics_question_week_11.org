** a
\begin{align*}
y_{\textrm{stub}} &= j\tan(\beta l) \\
y_{\textrm{stub}} &= j\tan(2\pi 0.206) \\
y_{\textrm{stub}} &= j3.5245 \\
z_{\textrm{source}} &= \frac{Z}{Z_0} \\
z_{\textrm{source}} &= \frac{50}{50} \\
z_{\textrm{source}} &= 1 \\
y_{\textrm{source}} &= \frac{1}{z_{\textrm{source}}} = 1 \\
y_A &= 1 + j3.5245 \\
z_A &= \frac{1}{1 + j3.5245} = 0.07450 - j0.26259 \\
\Gamma_A &= \frac{z_A - 1}{z_A + 1} \\
\Gamma_A &= \frac{0.07450 - j0.26259 - 1}{0.07450 - j0.26259 + 1} \\
\Gamma_A &= -0.75643 - j0.42924
\end{align*}

Transform through the $0.120 \lambda$ line:

\begin{align*}

\Gamma_S &= (-0.75643 - j0.42924) e^{-j2\cdot 2\pi 0.120\lambda} \\
\Gamma_S &= -0.4759 + j0.7280
\end{align*}
