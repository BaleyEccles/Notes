** Orbital Mechanics - Kepler's Laws
First Law:
 - The path followed by a satellite around the primary mass is elliptical
Second Law:
 - For equal time intervals, a satellite will sweep out equal areas in the orbital plane focused at the primary focal point
Third Law:
 - The square of the orbital period is proportional to the mean of the cube of the mean distance between the primary mass and the satellite
