* Noise
[[id:acdd879a-f336-4758-9a82-eb23901354f8][Noise Figure]]
 - $k = 1.38\times10^{-23}$
 - $T_0 = 290\ \textrm{K}$
** Thermal Noise
\[P_n = kTB\]
 - $T$ in Kelvin

** Equivalent Noise Temperature
\[T_e = \frac{N_0}{k B}\]

** Noisy Amplifier
\[N_0 = GkT_eB\]
 - When the source is noiseless
\[N_0 = GkB(T_s + T_e)\]
 - When the source has noise temperature $T_s$
   
** Y-Factor
Used to measure noise
\[N_1 = GkB(T_1 + T_e)\]
\[N_2 = GkB(T_2 + T_e)\]
The Y-factor is defined as:
\[Y = \frac{N_1}{N_2}\]
\[Y = \frac{T_1 + T_e}{T_2 + T_e}\]

** Non-Linear Distortion
Represented using [[id:356b1296-2188-4d04-9ccc-a4381bcc02b6][Taylor Series]] model:
\[v_0 = a_0 + a_1v_i + a_2v_i^2 + a_3v_i^3 + \hdots\]

*** Gain compression
Single-Tone Nonlinear Response:
\[v_i = V_0 \cos(\omega_0t)\]
\[G_v = a_1 + \frac{3}{4}a_3V_0^2\]

1 dB Compression Point:
\[OP_{1\textrm{dB}} = IP_{1\textrm{dB}} + G - 1\ \textrm{dB}\]

*** Two-Tone Intermodulation
\[v_i = V_0[\cos(\omega_1t) + \cos(\omega_2t)]\]
Frequencies of the form $m\omega_1 + n\omega_2$
 - $m,n = 0,\pm 1, \pm 2,\hdots$

*** Second-Order Products
Important second-order products are
\[2\omega_1\]
\[2\omega_2\]
\[\omega_1 - \omega_2\]
\[\omega_1 + \omega_2\]

*** Third-Order Products
\[3\omega_1\]
\[3\omega_2\]
\[2\omega_1 + \omega_2\]
\[\omega_1 + 2\omega_2\]
\[2\omega_1 - \omega_2\]
\[2\omega_2 - \omega_1\]

*** Linear Dynamic Range
\[LDR_{\textrm{dB}} = OP_{1\textrm{dB}} - N_o\]

*** Spurious-Free Dynamic Range
\[SFDR_{\textrm{dB}} = \frac{2}{3}(OIP3 - N_0 - SNR)\]
