* Question 1
\begin{align*}
IL &= 10\log_{10}(P_{LR}) \\
3 &= 10\log_{10}(1 + k^2) \\
k &= 0.997
\end{align*}

#+BEGIN_SRC octave :results output :exports none :session Q1 :eval no-export :tangle ENG439_Week_6_Q1.m

vpasolve(3 == 10*log10(1 + k^2), k)
#+END_SRC

#+RESULTS:
: Symbolic pkg v3.2.2: Python communication link active, SymPy v1.14.0.
: ans = (sym) 0.99762834511098350274928187753979

\begin{align*}
P_{LR}(N) &= 1 + k^2 T_N^2\left(\frac{\omega}{\omega_c}\right) \\
P_{LR}(N) &= 1 + (0.997)^2 T_N^2\left(\frac{5}{3}\right) \\
P_{LR}(2) &= 1 + (0.997)^2 T_2^2\left(\frac{5}{3}\right) \\
P_{LR}(2) &= 1 + (0.997)^2 \left(\left(\frac{5}{3}\right)^2 - 1\right)^2 \\
P_{LR}(2) &= 21.66 = 13.36\ \mathrm{dB} < 20\ \mathrm{dB}\\
P_{LR}(3) &= 1 + (0.997)^2 T_3^2\left(\frac{5}{3}\right) \\
P_{LR}(2) &= 1 + (0.997)^2 \left(4\left(\frac{5}{3}\right)^3 - 3\left(\frac{5}{3}\right)\right)^2 \\
P_{LR}(2) &= 182.9 = 22.62\ \mathrm{dB} > 20\ \mathrm{dB} \\
\therefore N = 3
\end{align*}

Using the $3\ \mathrm{dB}$ Chebyshev table we get:
\begin{align*}
g_1 &= 3.3487 \\
g_2 &= 0.7117 \\
g_3 &= 3.3487 \\
g_4 &= 1.0000
\end{align*}

\[L_1 \rightarrow C_2 \rightarrow L_3\]

\begin{align*}
L_k &= \frac{Z_0 g_k}{\omega_c} \\
L_1 &= \frac{75\cdot 3.3487}{2\pi 3\times10^9} \\
L_1 &= 13.32\ \matrm{nH} \\
L_3 &= \frac{75\cdot 3.3487}{2\pi 3\times10^9} \\
L_3 &= 13.32\ \matrm{nH} \\
C_k &= \frac{g_k}{Z_0\omega_c} \\
C_2 &= \frac{0.7117}{75\cdot 2\pi 3\times10^9} \\
C_2 &= 0.5\ \mathrm{pF}
\end{align*}

* Question 2
3rd order with equal-ripple response, so we can use the $3\ \mathrm{dB}$ Chebyshev table:
\begin{align*}
g_1 &= 3.3487 \\
g_2 &= 0.7117 \\
g_3 &= 3.3487 \\
g_4 &= 1.0000
\end{align*}


Using the high-pass transformation $$\omega \leftarrow -\frac{\omega_c}{\omega}$$, this converts inductors to capacitors and vice versa.
\[L_1 \rightarrow C_2 \rightarrow L_3 \Rightarrow C_1 \rightarrow L_2 \rightarrow C_3\]
\begin{align*}
C_k' &= \frac{1}{Z_0 \omega_c g_k} \\
C_1' &= 0.95\ \mathrm{pF} \\
C_3' &= 0.95\ \mathrm{pF} \\
L_k' &= \frac{Z_0}{\omega_c g_k} \\
L_2' &= 11.18 \mathrm{nH}
\end{align*}

* Question 3
Using the Maximally Flat Table with $N = 4$:
\begin{align*}
g_1 &= 1.0598 \\
g_2 &= 0.5116 \\
g_3 &= 0.3181 \\
g_4 &= 0.1104 \\
g_5 &= 1.0000 \\
\end{align*}

\begin{align*}
\Delta &= 5\% \\
\Delta &= \frac{\omega_2 - \omega_1}{\omega_0}
\end{align*}
For band-pass transformation inductors are replaced with series LC circuits:
\begin{align*}
L_k' &= \frac{Z_0g_k}{\Delta \omega_0} \\
C_k' &= \frac{\Delta}{Z_0 g_k \omega_0} \\
\end{align*}

Capacitors are replaced with parallel LC circuits:
\begin{align*}
L_k' &= \frac{Z_0\Delta}{g_k \omega_0} \\
C_k' &= \frac{g_k}{Z_0\Delta \omega_0} \\
\end{align*}

Using the following ~octave~ code we get:
