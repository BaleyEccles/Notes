* Question 1
** a
Terminate port 1 with $Z_S$:
\begin{align*}
V_1^+ &= \Gamma_S V_1^- \\
V_1^- &= S_{11} V_1^+ + S_{12} V_2^+ \\
V_1^- &= S_{11} \Gamma_S V_1^- + S_{12} V_2^+ \\
V_1^-(1 - S_{11}\Gamma_S) &= S_{12}V_2^+ \\
V_1^- &= \frac{S_{12}V_2^+}{(1 - S_{11}\Gamma_S)}
\end{align*}
Also:
\begin{align*}
V_1^+ &= \Gamma_S V_1^- \\
V_2^- &= S_{21} V_1^+ + S_{22} V_2^+ \\
V_2^- &= S_{21} \Gamma_S V_1^- + S_{22} V_2^+ \\
V_2^- &= S_{21} \Gamma_S \frac{S_{12}V_2^+}{(1 - S_{11}\Gamma_S)} + S_{22} V_2^+ \\
\Rightarrow \Gamma_{\textrm{out}} &= \frac{V_2^-}{V_2^+} = S_{22} + \frac{S_{12}S_{21}\Gamma_S}{1 - S_{11}\Gamma_S}
\end{align*}



** b
$\Gamma_{\textrm{out}}$ is the reflection coefficient looking into port 2. It describes:
\[Z_{\textrm{out}} = Z_0 \frac{1 + \Gamma_{\textrm{out}}}{1 - \Gamma_{\textrm{out}}}\]
It depends on $\Gamma_S$ because the amplifier can have reverse transmission through $S_{12}$.


** c
\begin{align*}
V_2^+ &= \Gamma_L V_2^- \\
V_2^- &= S_{21}V_1^+ + S_{22}V_2^+ \\
V_2^- &= S_{21}V_1^+ + S_{22}\Gamma_L V_2^- \\
\Rightarrow V_2^- &= \frac{S_{21}V_1^+}{1 - S_{22}\Gamma_L}
\end{align*}

** d
*** Input Power
\begin{align*}
P_{\textrm{in}} &= \frac{|V_1^+|^2}{2Z_0}(1 - |\Gamma_{in}|^2) \\
V_1^+ &= \frac{V_S}{2}\frac{1 - \Gamma_S}{1 - \Gamma_S \Gamma_{\textrm{in}}} \\
\lvert V_1^+\rvert^2 &= \frac{\sqrt{V_S^2}^2}{\sqrt{2^2}^2}\frac{\sqrt{(1 - \Gamma_S)^2}^2}{\sqrt{(1 - \Gamma_S \Gamma_{\textrm{in}})^2}^2} \\
\lvert V_1^+\rvert^2 &= \frac{|V_S|^2}{4}\frac{|1 - \Gamma_S|^2}{|1 - \Gamma_S \Gamma_{\textrm{in}}|^2} \\
\Rightarrow P_{\textrm{in}} &= \frac{\frac{|V_S|^2}{4}\frac{|1 - \Gamma_S|^2}{|1 - \Gamma_S \Gamma_{\textrm{in}}|^2}}{2Z_0}(1 - |\Gamma_{in}|^2) \\
P_{\textrm{in}} &= \frac{|V_S|^2}{8Z_0}\frac{|1-\Gamma_S|^2}{|1-\Gamma_S\Gamma_{\textrm{in}}|^2}(1-|\Gamma_{\textrm{in}}|^2)
\end{align*}

*** Power delivered to the load
\begin{align*}
P_L &= \frac{|V_2^-|^2}{2 Z_0} (1 - |\Gamma_L|^2) \\
V_2^- &= \frac{S_{21}}{1 - S_{22}\Gamma_L} V_1^+ \\
\lvert V_2^-\rvert^2 &= \frac{|S_{21}|^2}{|1 - S_{22}\Gamma_L|^2} |V_1^+|^2 \\
P_L &= \frac{\frac{|S_{21}|^2}{|1 - S_{22}\Gamma_L|^2} |V_1^+|^2}{2 Z_0} (1 - |\Gamma_L|^2) \\
P_L &= \frac{|V_1^+|^2}{2Z_0} \frac{|S_{21}|^2(1 - |\Gamma_L|^2)}{|1 - S_{22}\Gamma_L|^2}
\end{align*}

Sub in expression for $|V_1^+|^2$:
\[P_L = \frac{|V_S|^2}{8Z_0}\frac{|S_{21}|^2|1 - \Gamma_S|^2(1 - |\Gamma_L|^2)}{|1 - S_{22} \Gamma_L|^2 |1 - \Gamma_S \Gamma_{\textrm{in}}|^2}\]

*** Available power from the source ($P_{\textrm{avs}}$)
Maximum power transfer occurs when $\Gamma_{\textrm{in}} = \Gamma_S^{*}$:
\begin{align*}
P_{\textrm{in}} &= \frac{|V_S|^2}{8Z_0}\frac{|1-\Gamma_S|^2}{|1-\Gamma_S\Gamma_{\textrm{in}}|^2}(1-|\Gamma_{\textrm{in}}|^2) \\
P_{\textrm{avs}} &= \frac{|V_S|^2}{8Z_0}\frac{|1-\Gamma_S|^2}{|1-\Gamma_S\Gamma_S^{*}|^2}(1-|\Gamma_S^{*}|^2) \\
\Gamma_S\Gamma_S^{*} &= |\Gamma_S|^2 \qquad |\Gamma_S^{*}|^2 = |\Gamma_S|^2 \\
P_{\textrm{avs}} &= \frac{|V_S|^2}{8Z_0}\frac{|1-\Gamma_S|^2}{(1-|\Gamma_S|^2)^2}(1-|\Gamma_S|^2) \\
P_{\textrm{avs}} &= \frac{|V_S|^2}{8Z_0}\frac{|1-\Gamma_S|^2}{1-|\Gamma_S|^2} \\
\end{align*}

*** Difference between $P_{\textrm{avs}}$ and $P_{\textrm{in}}$
$P_{\textrm{avs}}$ is the maximum power that the source is capable of supplying and $P_{\textrm{in}}$ is the actual power entering the network.

*** Available power from the network $P_{\textrm{avn}}$
Maximum power transfer requires $\Gamma_L = \Gamma_{\textrm{out}}^{*}$. From:
\[P_L = \frac{|V_S|^2}{8Z_0}\frac{|S_{21}|^2|1 - \Gamma_S|^2(1 - |\Gamma_L|^2)}{|1 - S_{22} \Gamma_L|^2 |1 - \Gamma_S \Gamma_{\textrm{in}}|^2}\]
We can get:
\[P_{\textrm{avn}} = \frac{|V_S|^2}{8Z_0}\frac{|S_{21}|^2|1 - \Gamma_S|^2}{|1 - S_{11} \Gamma_L|^2 (1 - |\Gamma_{\textrm{out}}|^2)}\]

** e
\begin{align*}
G_A &= \frac{P_{\textrm{avn}}}{P_{\textrm{avs}}} \\
G_A &= \frac{\frac{|V_S|^2}{8Z_0}\frac{|S_{21}|^2|1 - \Gamma_S|^2}{|1 - S_{11} \Gamma_L|^2 (1 - |\Gamma_{\textrm{out}}|^2)}}{\frac{|V_S|^2}{8Z_0}\frac{|1-\Gamma_S|^2}{1-|\Gamma_S|^2}} \\
G_A &= \frac{|S_{21}|^2(1-|\Gamma_S|^2)}{|1 - S_{11}\Gamma_S|^2 (1 - |\Gamma_{\textrm{out}}|^2)}
\end{align*}

The initial substitution is the same as the lecture notes, however the final result is different.
