* Binary Symmetric Channel (BSC)
Bits $0\rightarrow 0$ (probability $\epsilon$) or $0\rightarrow 1$ (probability $1 - \epsilon$)
Bits $1\rightarrow 1$ (probability $\epsilon$) or $1\rightarrow 0$ (probability $1 - \epsilon$)
Perfect if $\epsilon = 0$, worst if $\epsilon = 0.5$
** How to find $\epsilon$
Assume binary antipodal signalling over [[id:0a3093d2-6df1-49f4-b00a-f506505c424d][AWGN]]
\[\epsilon = \Pr(1|0) = \Pr(0|1) = Q\left(\sqrt{\frac{2E_b}{N_0}}\right)\]
\[x = 0 \rightarrow -\sqrt{E_b}\]
\[x = 1 \rightarrow \sqrt{E_b}\]
\[\Pr(y|x) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}\sigma}e^{-\dfrac{(y - x)^2}{2\sigma^2}}\]
